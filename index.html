04 — UAE Impact

Building knowledge
in the UAE.

Advancing research, innovation and scientific capability.

Through research and education at Khalifa University, Dr. Rezeq contributes to the development of advanced scientific and technological capabilities in the United Arab Emirates.

His work spans nanotechnology, nanoelectronics, advanced materials, energy-related technologies and nanoscale sensing, connecting fundamental scientific research with potential technological applications.

His academic activities also contribute to the development of students and researchers, helping strengthen the UAE's growing research and innovation ecosystem.
