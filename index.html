01 — Profile

Science at the
nanoscale.

Researcher, inventor and educator working across physics, nanotechnology and advanced electronic systems.

Dr. Moh'd Rezeq is an Associate Professor of Physics at Khalifa University in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

His research focuses on nanotechnology, nanoelectronics, advanced materials, nanoscale characterization, nanofabrication and emerging energy-related technologies.

At Khalifa University, his work has included the establishment of nanofabrication and nanoprobe research capabilities capable of operating at the sub-1 nm scale.

His academic work combines fundamental physics with technological development, while also contributing to the education and supervision of students and researchers.

04 — United Arab Emirates

Building scientific
capability in the UAE.

Research, education and invention contributing to the UAE's knowledge economy.

Based at Khalifa University in Abu Dhabi, Dr. Rezeq's work contributes to the UAE's research and innovation environment through advanced physics research, nanotechnology and technological development.

His research activities include areas connected with energy storage, energy harvesting, nanoelectronics, sensors, quantum-dot devices and advanced materials.

His laboratory and research activities also contribute to scientific infrastructure and advanced research capabilities within the UAE.

Through the supervision and training of students and researchers, his academic work supports the development of scientific and engineering expertise.
